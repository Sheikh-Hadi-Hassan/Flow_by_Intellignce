# ADR-037: BLM Expands Through Universal Business Expertise Domains

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Previous BLM chapters established semantic contracts, component registry,
business brain foundation, governed acquisition, human review, provenance,
knowledge releases, and publication gates.

The next step is broader operational business understanding before any model
runtime is introduced.

## Decision

Expand BLM with 15 universal business expertise domains rather than hundreds of
industry-specific brains.

The expansion is Flow-curated knowledge in `@flow/blm-contracts`, covering
concepts, relationships, processes, skills, formulas, rules, metrics,
psychology insights, diagnostic patterns, decision patterns, cross-domain
relationships, deterministic evaluation cases, and measurable expertise scores.

## Consequences

BLM gains broader business understanding while preserving the rule that
knowledge is not authority. Future execution still requires roles,
permissions, Action Wall, approvals, and audit.

The expansion does not claim regulatory, legal, payroll, manufacturing, or
jurisdiction-specific completeness.

## Alternatives Considered

- Add many industry-specific packs now: rejected because universal packs plus
  thin overlays scale better.
- Integrate an LLM before expanding structured knowledge: rejected because
  model reasoning should consume governed expertise, not invent it.
- Build ERP modules: rejected because this chapter is knowledge only.
