# BLM Business Expertise Expansion v1

## Purpose

BLM Business Expertise Expansion v1 broadens Flow's structured business
knowledge from proof packs into 15 composable universal expertise domains. The
goal is operational understanding: concepts, relationships, processes, skills,
rules, metrics, psychology, diagnostics, decision factors, cross-domain effects,
and deterministic evaluation cases.

This chapter does not add an LLM, RAG, embeddings, model training, ERP runtime,
workflow engine, accounting engine, CRM database, HR system, marketplace
backend, or manufacturing execution.

## Universal Domain Model

The expansion defines these domain tracks:

- universal-core
- crm-sales

Existing proof packs are extended, not duplicated.

## Marketing And Growth

Marketing is first-class BLM expertise. It models markets, segments,
audiences, personas, positioning, offers, campaigns, channels, attribution,
conversion, funnels, acquisition, retention, churn, experiments, and cohorts.

Deterministic formulas and metrics include CAC, LTV, ROAS, conversion rate,
retention, churn, and payback period.

## Business Psychology

Customer and organizational psychology are represented as structured business
insights, not manipulation tooling. Insights contain observed signals,
alternative interpretations, ethical responses, risks, confidence category, and
prohibited misuse.

BLM must not infer a single motive automatically and must not encode coercion,
deception, exploitation, hidden manipulation, discriminatory targeting, or
mental-health diagnosis.

## Cross-Domain Graph

The expansion explicitly models business flow across domains:

Marketing Campaign -> Lead -> Opportunity -> Quote -> Contract/Order ->
Fulfillment -> Invoice -> Receivable -> Payment.

It also links product cost to COGS, revenue/COGS to gross profit, project
revenue/cost to project margin, procurement to inventory and payables, assets
to depreciation expense, marketplace payments to commission and payout, and MRP
material requirements to inventory availability.

## Diagnostic Patterns

Diagnostic patterns are deterministic investigation maps, not inference
engines. v1 includes:

- Sales Decline
- Negative Cash Flow
- Low Project Margin
- High Churn

Each pattern lists investigation concepts, metrics, domains, and prohibited
unsupported conclusions.

## Decision Patterns

Decision patterns expose factors without making automatic decisions. v1
includes:

- Discount Decision
- Ethical Persuasion Boundary
- Cash Preservation Decision

These patterns identify inputs and decision factors while preserving human,
policy, or deterministic-engine authority.

## Domain Maturity Scoring

`DomainExpertiseScore` is deterministic and based on measurable registry
contents:

- concept coverage
- relationship coverage
- process coverage
- skill coverage
- rule/formula coverage
- metric coverage
- evaluation coverage
- cross-domain integration

Scores are evidence of structured coverage, not claims of complete expertise.

## Evaluation Expansion

The expansion defines more than 50 deterministic evaluation cases. Critical
cross-domain cases cover:

- profitable but cash-flow negative
- lead count rising while revenue falls
- utilization rising while project margin falls
- inventory exists but order cannot be fulfilled
- customer requests a discount

No LLM calls are involved.

## 90 Percent Coverage Strategy

Flow should cover normal business operations through universal domain packs,
BusinessProfile, ISIC-style classification, thin industry overlays, and
workspace configuration. This avoids hundreds of isolated industry brains while
still enabling specialization.

## Small Business Capability Target

The expanded BLM can reason about common small-business operations: leads,
quotes, invoices, payments, pricing, inventory availability, vendor purchases,
projects, basic HR, assets, marketplace flows, and KPI interpretation.

It is not yet an execution system.

## Enterprise Capability Target

Enterprise-oriented coverage begins with structured finance, procurement,
project/service operations, commercial documents, analytics, and MRP
foundations. Compliance, jurisdictional accounting/tax, workforce planning,
legal approval, and advanced manufacturing remain future work.

## Known Gaps

- jurisdiction-specific tax/accounting compliance
- legal advice and legal approval
- payroll compliance
- complete manufacturing/MRP expertise
- industry overlays
- real workflow/runtime execution
- deterministic formula engine implementation
- business context compiler
- model reasoning layer
